## Release Decision

## Delivered Contract

The existing `GET /api/v1/suppliers/{supplier_id}/scorecard` remains v1-compatible and attaches
the latest persisted v2 detail when available. Seven new routes provide recomputation, latest-risk
listing, brief preparation/list/detail, and append-only acknowledge/dismiss actions. Together,
OpenAPI exposes all eight approved R4.1 route/method pairs.

- Active tenant members may read scorecards, risk snapshots, and briefs.
- Only owners and buyers may recompute, prepare, acknowledge, or dismiss.
- Mutations require a UUID `Idempotency-Key` at runtime and append an audit event.
- Cross-tenant identifiers are indistinguishable from unknown identifiers (`404 not_found`).
- Lists use opaque cursors with a default of 50 and maximum of 100.
- Every v2 response exposes `release_posture = "g3_unmet"`.

## Business Validation

The deterministic risk model uses a 180-day window split into equal baseline/current halves and
`Decimal` arithmetic throughout. The four components are concentration (30%), price drift (25%),
reliability (25%), and single-source exposure (20%). Exactly three available components are
renormalized; fewer than three suppress the score. Risk bands are low below 0.35, medium from 0.35
to below 0.65, and high at or above 0.65.

Concentration stays in currency buckets. Price drift compares product/unit/currency medians and
excludes zero baselines. Reliability includes due non-cancelled orders, uses cumulative line
receipts, reports exclusions, and measures current failure plus decay. Single-source exposure
requires a compatible active alternative for the same normalized product, unit, and currency.

Briefs contain at most one deterministic item in each category: price trajectory, alternatives,
service performance, concentration/volume, payment context, and purchase pattern. Calculations,
validity, confidence, explicit money currencies, localized question keys, and typed source links
are stored or returned; generated prose is not used. Missing due dates or provider balances remain
unavailable rather than inferred.

## Data and Isolation

Migration `20260920000009_supplier_iq_v2.sql` extends the existing snapshot and synced-bill rows
and adds normalized metric, evidence, brief, item, item-evidence, and action tables. All new tables
have forced RLS, tenant-pinned foreign keys, select `USING` and insert `WITH CHECK` policies, and
no update/delete/truncate grants. Snapshot, brief, and action writes are transactional; evidence
links are typed and each brief item must have evidence before commit.
