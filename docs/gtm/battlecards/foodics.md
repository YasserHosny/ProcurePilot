# Battlecard: Foodics

**Last verified**: 2026-10-28 (foodics.com: homepage, KSA pricing, Supply page; Foodics × Suplyd release, 2023-11-08) · **First version**

## When you'll see them
In almost every Egyptian and Saudi account, **as the POS**. Foodics shows support lines for KSA, UAE, Egypt, Kuwait and Jordan, and claims "40,000+ restaurants". It rarely comes up as a purchasing competitor. It comes up as "Foodics already has a purchasing screen."

## Why buyers mention them
It's already installed and paid for. Inventory "Costing & Purchasing" is included from the **Basic** bundle (450/mo, about 414/mo billed annually; currency not shown on the KSA page, likely SAR). Foodics Supply and the Suplyd partnership put sourcing inside it.

## Where they're strong (don't argue these)
- System of record for sales, menu and payments; Arabic-native; a large regional footprint
- Purchasing is "free" inside the bundle, and the data sits next to sales
- Ecosystem: payments (SAMA-licensed), financing (Capital), App Marketplace, Suplyd integration in Egypt

## How to position: complement, not competitor
"Foodics is your till. We're your purchasing desk." We don't replace the POS, and we shouldn't try.

## Where we can win, and for whom (hypotheses)
| Hypothesis | Who it fits | What would confirm it |
|---|---|---|
| Foodics purchasing records what you ordered; it doesn't collect and compare competing quotes (not observed on their site) | Groups that re-quote key items weekly or monthly | Buyer compares quotes outside Foodics today |
| Branch → approval → PO with budgets per branch and cost centre matters to groups with a central purchasing role | 3+ branches with a purchasing manager or owner approval | Buyer describes approvals over WhatsApp |
| Savings per decision is a number Foodics doesn't produce | Owners and finance | Buyer can't say what purchasing saved last month |

## Discovery questions
1. "Do you use the purchasing screen in Foodics today? For which items?"
2. "Before you enter an order there, how do you decide the supplier and price?"
3. "Who approves a branch's order above a certain amount?"

## Objections
| They say | Answer (one line) | Proof / next step |
|---|---|---|
| "Foodics already does purchasing." | "It records the purchase. We help with the step before: getting and comparing the quotes." | Demo: RFQ → quotes → compare |
| "Does it integrate with Foodics?" | "Not yet. In the pilot, stock levels come from branch staff reporting low stock in our mobile app, so the POS isn't needed." | Honest gap; log it as `from-pilot` if it blocks |
| "We'd rather wait for Foodics to add it." | "Reasonable. The pilot is 14 days and free, so you'll know the size of the saving either way." | Pilot terms |

## When to walk away
- The buyer requires a Foodics integration before any trial.
- One branch, owner buys everything personally: there's no approval or comparison workflow to improve.

## Follow-up for us (not the rep)
A Foodics integration or App Marketplace listing would turn this card from defence into distribution. Raise it as a product decision; it's not on the roadmap today.
