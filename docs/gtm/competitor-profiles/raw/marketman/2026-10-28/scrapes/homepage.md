Frequently Asked Questions
Say goodbye to time-consuming inventory management tasks.
What is MarketMan and how does it improve restaurant profitability?
+
MarketMan is a cloud-based restaurant management platform that centralizes inventory control, purchasing, expense tracking, and recipe costing in one place. By automating back-of-house tasks, restaurants typically reduce COGS by 3–5% and reclaim hours previously lost to manual processes.
What types of restaurants use MarketMan?
+
Quick-service restaurants, full-service dining, cafes, bars, and ghost kitchens all use MarketMan to manage back-of-house operations. The platform is designed to perform for single-location operators and large multi-unit chains alike, with features that adapt to the specific needs of each business model.
How long does it take to implement MarketMan?
+
Implementation typically takes 2–4 weeks for single-location restaurants, with guided onboarding, data migration, and staff training provided by the MarketMan team from day one. The process is designed to minimize operational disruption and get teams productive as quickly as possible.
How much does MarketMan cost?
+
Tier-based pricing starts at $249/month for the Starter plan, with Growth ($299/month) and Enterprise (from $449) options available for operations with more complex needs. Visit the Pricing Page or contact the sales team for a quote tailored to your restaurant group.
Can I see a demo of MarketMan before committing?
+
A free, no-obligation demo is available with a dedicated MarketMan restaurant expert who will walk through the platform and answer questions specific to your operation. Request one directly from the MarketMan website at any time.
