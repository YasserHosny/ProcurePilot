Supy vs Spreadsheets
Spreadsheets are analysis tools, only as good as the numbers somebody typed in - stock count and invoice data entered by hand, with sales pasted from the POS. Every manual step is a chance to get it wrong. Supy does the work instead: stock counts happen on a phone, AI reads your supplier invoices, and every sale automatically depletes stock. Ordering and receiving run in Supy too, so you always know what you are spending. Nobody types the numbers in, so everything stays up to date automatically. And when you want a spreadsheet, you can export to Excel in one click.

Can Supy replace our inventory spreadsheet, and do we lose our Excel reports?
+
Yes to the first, no to the second. The change is not that you get a better spreadsheet - it is that nobody types the numbers in. Staff do stock counts on a phone by scanning barcodes, instead of writing them down and typing them up afterwards. AI reads your supplier invoices. Every sale automatically takes its ingredients out of stock. Ordering and receiving happen in Supy too, so you can see what you are spending with each supplier as it happens.
Because the numbers are captured as the work is done, the analysis is already there: live stock, food cost per site, and which dishes actually make you money - and you can click any figure to see what caused it.
Your Excel work does not disappear. Supy exports to Excel from every page and includes a Spreadsheet Reports module, so anything your finance team has built can carry on running, against current data instead of data somebody retyped.
What is Supy?
Supy is an inventory management platform built to help hospitality businesses maximize their profits by reducing their costs. Our suite of products cover all back of house operations, namely procurement, inventory, invoices & settlements, central kitchen, production planning, menu & recipes.
