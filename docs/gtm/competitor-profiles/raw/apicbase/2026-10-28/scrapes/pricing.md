Frequently asked questions
Can I opt to move to a higher tier when we expand?
Yes, you can upgrade your plan anytime. Just get in touch with us and we’ll take it from there to upgrade your account with additional outlets and/or modules.
How do I end the subscription?
In order to end your subscription, please get in touch with your account manager or CS manager prior to the renewal date. We’ll make sure that the subscription won’t be renewed automatically.
When you cancel your subscription, there’s a grace period in which you can still access your account to download all the necessary data.
How will my company get billed?
Subscriptions are billed annually and via credit card. If you choose for monthly billing, an additional fee of 15% will apply.
Am I eligible for a discount?
The listed pricing is for the start with 1 location. When you add more locations a quantity discount is applicable. Meaning the fee per location will drop the more locations you add.
