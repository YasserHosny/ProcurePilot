Frequently Asked Questions
Does Apicbase track stock?
Yes, POS integration enables ingredient tracking and    stock updates   , down to individual raw materials. Updates happen nightly to ensure ops stability.
Can outlets order directly from suppliers through Apicbase?
Yes, outlets can    place orders with suppliers    directly via Apicbase. They can also order from multiple suppliers at once.
Does Apicbase support barcode scanning for inventory counts and order intake?
Yes, the Apicbase mobile app supports barcode and QR code scanning, updating stock instantly with every scan.
Does Apicbase track ingredient usage in semifinished products or batch-made components such as sauces?
Yes, Apicbase tracks all stock movements. This includes when ingredients are used to create semi-finished products or sub-recipes, such as sauces. Stock is depleted during production and then returned to inventory as meal components.
Does Apicbase suggest purchase orders?
Yes, Apicbase recommends what outlets should order from suppliers. It analyses demand, current stock levels, lead times, and outstanding orders to generate purchase orders for each supplier. Suggestions can always be adjusted.
Does Apicbase automatically update ingredient prices?
Yes, when suppliers integrate their catalogues with Apicbase, the system automatically updates ingredient prices.
Does Apicbase provide invoice matching?
Yes, Apicbase uses three-way matching for invoices (Purchase Order, Goods receipt/Delivery note and Invoice)
What level of support does Apicbase provide?
Apicbase offers full support, including    onboarding   , a    Help Centre   , an    Academy   , and live chat support managed by our team (no bots).
Is user access role-dependent?
Yes, Apicbase offers detailed permission settings to ensure users see only relevant data and options based on their roles and/or locations.
How does Apicbase keeps our data secure?
Apicbase is  SOC II, Type 2  compliant.
The software is built with security at its core to meet the expectations of modern, multi-site foodservice organisations.
We apply proven, industry-standard controls across data protection, access management, infrastructure, and integrations, and these are continuously monitored and pressure-tested by independent, ISO-certified companies.
At a glance:
Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest
Access is governed by role-based access control (RBAC) with audit logging
Secure authentication options, including Single Sign-On (SSO), are supported
Customer data is logically isolated per tenant
Infrastructure is continuously monitored, backed up, and protected by disaster recovery processes
Integrations are secured via MCP (Master Connection Platform) — the gold standard for hospitality and foodservice connectivity — reducing integration risk and attack surface
