## Positioning & Messaging

A POS-first restaurant platform that has grown into an ecosystem: POS, payments (Foodics Pay, licensed by SAMA), online ordering, KDS, accounting, BI, Capital (financing) and **Foodics Supply** ("Source everything you need in one place—control costs and inventory from Foodics").

## Product & Features (procurement-relevant)

- Inventory "Costing & Purchasing" is included from the **Basic** bundle up
- **Foodics Supply**: sourcing from inside Foodics. The KSA campaign landing page we fetched showed only generic POS copy, so details were not observed.
- **Egypt: partnership with Suplyd (Nov 2023)**: "integrate Suplyd's procurement platform with Foodics' restaurant management system". Phase 1 covers ordering and reordering; phase 2 automates replenishment.
- App Marketplace for third-party integrations

**Not observed (2026-10-28):** RFQs or comparing competing supplier quotes.

## Strengths & Weaknesses

**Strengths**: it's the system of record in many target accounts (it's the POS). It's Arabic-native, has a large regional footprint, and has a distribution and financing ecosystem.
**Weaknesses (inferred)**: procurement is a feature inside a POS, not the core product. In Egypt its sourcing story is delegated to a marketplace partner (Suplyd).

## Competitive Implications for ProcurePilot

- **Treat it as a likely integration, not a head-on competitor.** Many Egyptian and Saudi prospects will run Foodics POS. ProcurePilot's POS integration today is Square only (a known fit gap in `first-customer-gtm-plan.md`). A Foodics connector, or an App Marketplace listing, could unblock adoption.
- **Threat:** Foodics bundles "good-enough" purchasing for free in Basic. Buyers may ask "why another tool?" The answer has to be savings per sourcing decision, which a POS doesn't measure.
