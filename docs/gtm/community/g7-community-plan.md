# G-7 community plan: warm introductions in Egypt

**Written**: 2026-10-30 · **For**: Dr. Yasser · **Jira**: PPS-115 (P-84), feeds G-7 (PPS-20)
**Built with**: the `community-marketing` skill (installed 2026-10-30). That skill is about building your own community. This plan uses its member-first rules to **join** existing communities, which is what G-7 needs at zero budget.

## The goal, in one line
Turn people you already know, and communities you show up in, into **introductions to named Wave 1 accounts** (`docs/gtm/prospects/`, local only). An introduction is worth more than any number of cold messages. The Wave 1 notes rank "a mutual introduction" as the best way into every account.

**Target by 2026-11-26 (four weeks):** 20 Leads in SP (G-7's exit condition), at least 8 of them reached through a person, not a cold message.

## Rules
- **Organic only.** No paid boosts, sponsorships or bought lists.
- **Member first.** Don't pitch in any group. You join, help, and then people ask what you do.
- **Nothing goes out without owner approval:** no post, DM or comment. Drafts go to the owner first.
- **No scraping** of group members or attendee lists. A person enters SP only after a real exchange, or when a mutual contact names them.
- Same facts as the battlecards and the DM script: quotes come in by forward or upload (no direct WhatsApp connection); demo numbers are demo numbers; no savings claims.

## Who you are in these communities (the identity)
Not "a vendor". **An Egyptian founder building software for restaurant groups, who wants to understand how purchasing really works.** That's true, it's interesting to F&B owners, and it gives you a reason to ask questions without selling.

## Track 1 — Personal network audit (week 1, highest yield)

Go through your phone contacts, WhatsApp groups, LinkedIn connections, university, Iron Systems clients and family. Fill this table locally in `docs/gtm/prospects/` (it holds named people and companies, and the repo is public; that folder is gitignored):

| Person | How you know them | Works in or knows F&B? | Which Wave 1 account could they reach? | The ask | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | Owner / ops / chef / supplier / investor / none | A named Wave 1 account (see the local list) or "any restaurant group" | Intro / 10-min reaction / nothing yet | |

**Who to look for first:**
1. Anyone who **owns or runs** a restaurant, café, bakery or cloud kitchen with 2+ branches.
2. **Suppliers** to restaurants (food distributors, packaging, cleaning). They know every buyer in their area.
3. **Chefs and F&B consultants.** They move between groups and know who handles purchasing.
4. **Investors and accountants** who work with F&B groups (several Wave 1 accounts are investor-backed).

**The ask** (DM script §2 covers the wording). Ask for one of two things only:
- «تعرف حد بيدير مشتريات لسلسلة مطاعم؟ عايز ١٠ دقايق أفهم منه بيشتغلوا إزاي، مش ببيع حاجة.» / "Do you know someone who runs purchasing for a restaurant group? I'd like 10 minutes to understand how they work. I'm not selling anything."
- If they're the buyer themselves: the diagnostic question from the DM script.

Make it easy to forward: one short paragraph they can paste, plus the 60-second video link. Thank everyone who introduces you, whatever happens next.

## What to give (before asking for anything)
Things you can share without selling, all true and all free:
1. **A one-page "how to compare supplier quotes fairly"** guide: per pack vs. per kilo, delivery fees, VAT included or not. Useful to any buyer, whatever tool they use. *(Draft for owner approval; no product pitch inside.)*
2. **Answers in threads** about costing, supplier comparison and approvals, from what the demo-tenant rounds taught you.
3. **What you're learning** from discovery calls, with nobody named: "Five restaurant groups told me their biggest purchasing headache is…" Only after there *are* five calls (PPS-116).

## Weekly cadence (about 4 hours)
| Day | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday | Personal network: 5 new asks or follow-ups | 45 min |
| Monday–Wednesday | 2 communities: read, reply to 3 threads where you can help | 20 min a day |
| One evening | One event or mixer when available | 2 h |
| Thursday | Update SP; review what worked; draft next week's posts for approval | 30 min |

## From conversation to SP Lead
| What happened | Do this |
|---|---|
| Someone offers an introduction to a named company | Create the SP Lead at **Target** with "Source: intro via [first name]" and the introducer as a note |
| The introduction is made | Move to **Contacted**; first message within 24 hours |
| A real two-way conversation | Move to **Talked**; book the discovery call ([call script](../outreach/discovery-demo-call-script.md)) |
| A community member asks what you do | Answer in one line; offer the video in DM, not in the thread |

## How we'll know it's working (review every Thursday)
- Introductions offered this week (target: 2+ from week 2).
- SP Leads with an "intro via" source vs. cold.
- Conversations at **Talked** (G-9 counts these).
- Warning sign: four weeks of community time and no introduction. Then move hours back to the personal network and events, and drop the online groups.
