# Discovery and demo call script (AR + EN)

**Written**: 2026-10-30 · **For**: Dr. Yasser, founder-led calls · **Jira**: PPS-114 (P-83)
**Sits after**: the [Outreach DM Script](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/30523eee-168f-4a1c-b7ac-dcb50cd9ded8) and the [cold email sequence](cold-email-sequence.md). Objections: the [WhatsApp + Excel battlecard](../battlecards/whatsapp-excel.md), plus the [Supy](../battlecards/supy.md), [Suplyd](../battlecards/suplyd.md) and [Foodics](../battlecards/foodics.md) cards.

Two calls, not one. **Discovery first, demo second.** If the buyer insists on seeing it now, do a 10-minute discovery, then show only the two workflows that match what they said.

| Call | Length | Goal | Move the SP Lead to |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Discovery | 20–30 min | Is there pain, and do they meet the five pilot criteria? | **Talked** (always), **Qualified** (if all five are true) |
| 2. Demo | 30 min | Show their pain solved on the demo workspace; agree a pilot | **Pilot offered** |

**Before every call** (5 min): read the SP Lead and the Wave 1 row; pick the one observation you'll open with; open the demo workspace («مطاعم سند» for Egypt, «مطاعم رواق» for KSA) in a clean browser tab; have the battlecard open.

**Rules on every call**
- Every figure on screen is **demonstration data**. Say so the first time a number appears: «الأرقام دي تجريبية» / "these are demo numbers" (C-14).
- Never state a savings %, a time saved, or a customer. There are none yet.
- WhatsApp: always "you forward or upload it", never "it connects to WhatsApp" (C-10).
- Mobile approves **requests**; the PO is made on the web.
- Write the buyer's exact words in the SP Lead comment the same day.

## Call 1 — Discovery (20–30 min)

### 1. Open (2 min)
**AR**
> شكرًا إنك اديتني الوقت ده. أنا مش هعرض حاجة النهارده، عايز أفهم إنتو بتشتروا إزاي. حوالي ٢٠ دقيقة، وفي الآخر لو شايفين إن في حاجة تستاهل، أوريكم ٣٠ دقيقة في مكالمة تانية. ينفع؟

**EN**
> Thanks for the time. I won't show you anything today; I want to understand how you buy. About 20 minutes, and at the end, if there's something worth it, I'll show you in 30 minutes on a second call. OK?

### 2. How they buy today (8 min)
Ask, then stay quiet. Follow up on anything with a number in it.
1. «عندكم كام فرع، وفي مطبخ مركزي أو مخزن؟» / "How many branches, and is there a central kitchen or store?"
2. «بتشتروا من كام مورد في الأسبوع تقريبًا؟ وإيه أكتر أصناف بتصرفوا عليها؟» / "Roughly how many suppliers a week? Which items do you spend most on?"
3. «مين بيطلب: الفروع ولا حد مركزي؟ ومين بيوافق؟» / "Who orders: the branches or someone central? Who approves?"
4. «العروض بتوصلكم إزاي؟ صورة على الواتساب، PDF، مكالمة؟» / "How do quotes arrive? A WhatsApp photo, a PDF, a call?"
5. «وبعد ما توصل، بتتقارن فين؟» / "Once they arrive, where are they compared?"

### 3. The cost of today (8 min)
Use the battlecard's discovery questions 2, 3, 5 and 6. The one that matters most:
> «لو سألتك دلوقتي: مين أرخص مورد للزيت الأسبوع اللي فات؟ تعرف تقول من غير ما تفتح الواتساب؟»
> "If I asked you now who was cheapest on oil last week, could you say without opening WhatsApp?"

Then quantify, in their words, not yours:
- «ده بياخد من وقت مين، وكام ساعة في الأسبوع تقريبًا؟» / "Whose time does this take, and roughly how many hours a week?"
- «حصل قبل كده إنكم اكتشفتوا إنكم دفعتوا أغلى من اللازم؟ احكيلي.» / "Has it happened that you found you paid more than you needed to? Tell me about it."

### 4. Decision and timing (3 min)
- «لو لقيتوا حاجة تحل ده، مين غيرك لازم يوافق؟» / "If you found something that fixes this, who else would need to agree?"
- «في حاجة جاية تخلي ده أهم دلوقتي؟ فرع جديد، مورد جديد، موسم؟» / "Anything coming that makes this more urgent? A new branch, a new supplier, a season?"

### 5. Close (2 min)
**If there's pain:**
> «اللي سمعته: [ملخص بكلامهم]. ممكن ٣٠ دقيقة أوريكم ده على مثال لمطعم زيكم، يوم [يوم] الساعة [ساعة]؟ ولو ينفع، [اسم المسؤول عن الشراء] يحضر.»
> "What I heard: [summary in their words]. Can I take 30 minutes to show you this on a restaurant example like yours, [day] at [time]? If possible, bring [buyer's name]."

**If there's no pain:** thank them, ask «تعرف حد عنده المشكلة دي؟» / "Do you know someone who has this problem?", and move the Lead to **Not now** with the reason.

### Qualification check (fill in after the call)
| Criterion | Yes / No | Evidence (their words) |
|---|---|---|
| 2+ branches (or 1 + central kitchen/store) | | |
| 5+ suppliers, weekly orders | | |
| Quotes collected by hand today | | |
| A named decision-maker who'll attend two 30-min check-ins | | |
| Will share last month's prices on 10–20 items | | |

All five → **Qualified**. Four → book the demo anyway and ask about the missing one there.

## Call 2 — Demo (30 min)

Show **three workflows**, each tied to something they said on call 1. Skip any they didn't raise.

### 1. Open and recap (3 min)
> «المرة اللي فاتت قلتلي [ألم ١] و[ألم ٢]. النهارده هوريك الاتنين دول بس على مثال تجريبي، والأرقام اللي هتشوفها تجريبية. وقفني في أي وقت.»
> "Last time you told me [pain 1] and [pain 2]. Today I'll show just those two, on a demo example; the numbers you'll see are demo numbers. Stop me any time."

### 2. Workflow A — Quotes in, side by side (10 min) · for "comparing takes time" / "prices are in photos"
1. **RFQ**: build one request for 4 items, pick 2 suppliers, **Send**. «إرسال الطلب دايمًا بإيد حد عندكم» / "Sending is always someone's action" (C-16).
2. **A supplier's quote arrives** as a PDF or photo (show the Quotation Inbox). «المورد بعت زي ما هو متعود؛ إنت حولتها لنا» / "The supplier sent it as usual; you forwarded it to us."
3. **Review**: the quote beside its extracted lines, each line linked to its place in the document. «النظام بيعلّم اللي مش متأكد منه، وحد عندكم يراجع» / "It flags what it's unsure of, and someone on your side checks" (C-02).
4. **Compare responses**: line by line, pack for pack, delivery included; the basket split if one supplier is cheaper on some items (C-03, C-04).

*Interaction point:* «ده شبه اللي بيحصل عندكم، ولا الموردين بيبعتوا بشكل مختلف؟» / "Does this look like yours, or do your suppliers send it differently?"

### 3. Workflow B — Branch request, approved on the phone (7 min) · for "branches order on their own"
1. A purchase request is prepared from the winning response (with the guardrail on, it's a **draft**; nothing is bought).
2. The approver sees it on the phone with the remaining budget, and approves (C-06, C-09).
3. The purchase order is generated on the web from the approved request.

*Interaction point:* «مين عندكم هيبقى الشخص اللي بيوافق؟ وعلى أي مبلغ؟» / "Who would approve, and above what amount?"

### 4. Workflow C — The saving, verified (3 min) · for "how would I know it saved?"
Record the outcome from the PO; show the savings ledger. Say it out loud:
> «الرقم ده تجريبي. في التجربة بتاعتكم، التوفير بيتحسب بس لما حد عندكم يأكده، ومقارنة بأسعار الشهر اللي فات.»
> "This number is a demo. In your trial, a saving only counts when someone on your side confirms it, against last month's prices" (C-07).

### 5. Pilot offer and close (7 min)
**AR**
> «اقتراحي: تجربة مجانية ١٤ يوم على ١٠ لـ٢٠ صنف من اللي بتشتروه كل أسبوع. أنا بعمل الإعداد بنفسي، ومن ناحيتكم مكالمتين ٣٠ دقيقة. قبل ما نبدأ بنسجل أسعار الشهر اللي فات، وفي الآخر بنعرف التوفير بالجنيه. لو مكملتوش، بنسلمكم بياناتكم ونمسحها. أبعتلك الاتفاق (صفحة واحدة) النهارده؟»

**EN**
> "My suggestion: a free 14-day trial on 10 to 20 items you buy every week. I do the setup; your side is two 30-minute calls. Before we start we record last month's prices, and at the end we know the saving in pounds. If you don't continue, you get your data back and we delete it. Shall I send the one-page agreement today?"

If they ask the price after the trial (Egypt only; **KSA has no price yet**, say "free 14-day trial" only):
> EGP 1,500 per branch per month, minimum 2 branches. If verified savings in a month are under 3× the fee, the next month is free. Founding customers get 50% off for the first 12 months in exchange for a case study and logo (D-2).

**Before hanging up**, agree: the agreement goes out today; who signs; the date for week-0 setup; who joins the support WhatsApp group.

### Gaps to admit if asked (don't hide them)
| They ask | Honest answer |
|---|---|
| "Does it connect to WhatsApp?" | No. You forward the photo or PDF by email, or upload it (C-10). |
| "Does it work with Foodics / our POS / Odoo?" | Not yet. The trial doesn't need it; you get CSV exports for your accountant. |
| "Can it compare a USD quote with an EGP one?" | Not yet; one currency per comparison. The trial uses suppliers who quote in EGP (C4). |
| "Can I approve the PO on my phone?" | You approve the **request** on the phone; the PO is made on the web. |
| "Is VAT included?" | For the trial we compare prices ex-VAT, so savings aren't overstated (C5). |

## After every call (same day)
1. SP Lead: stage, a comment with the buyer's exact words on pain, objections and next step, and the due date.
2. A new objection that isn't on a battlecard → add it to the card's table with "(heard once, [date])".
3. A product gap that would block a pilot → a PPS task labelled `from-demo`.
4. Every call: finish `docs/gtm/research/calls/YYYY-MM-DD-<company-slug>.md` from the [template](../research/call-notes-template.md) within 24 hours (local only, gitignored; PPS-116).
