# R3.3 Order Tracking and Three-Way Match Design

**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Roadmap release:** R3.3, Phase 3 Connected Operations  
**Provider decision:** Xero is the second accounting provider after QuickBooks Online.

## Goal

Extend ProcurePilot from bill reconciliation to end-to-end purchasing reconciliation without
writing to external systems:

`Purchase Order -> Supplier Confirmation -> Delivery Receipt -> Accounting Bill`

The release must show where ordered, confirmed, received, and invoiced values agree or diverge,
while keeping purchasing and approval decisions human-authorized.

## Scope

### Included

- A provider-agnostic Xero connector using the existing accounting connector boundary.
- Normalized purchase-order and order-line records for orders created or recorded in ProcurePilot.
- Supplier confirmation records linked to purchase orders.
- Delivery receipt records and received quantities by order line.
- Three-way match records linking order, receipt, and accounting bill evidence.
- Discrepancies for quantity, price, currency, timing, missing confirmation, missing receipt, and
  invoice-without-order cases.
- Read-only provider synchronization, encrypted OAuth credentials, advisory-lock concurrency, and
  append-only audit events.
- Review surfaces that let owners and buyers investigate and resolve discrepancies.

### Excluded

- Creating, changing, sending, or cancelling orders in Xero or QuickBooks.
- Autonomous purchasing, payment, approval, or supplier communication.
- Forecasting, reorder proposals, negotiation briefs, or supplier-risk modelling from Phase 4.
- A full supplier portal. Supplier confirmations may be entered or imported by authorized users
  until a later supplier-facing workflow is justified.
- A second POS or inventory provider; R3.2 remains the source for POS usage signals.

## User Outcomes

1. A buyer can see whether a purchase order was confirmed, delivered, and invoiced.
2. A buyer can identify quantity or price variance without cross-referencing multiple screens.
3. An owner can inspect an invoice that has no corresponding purchase order.
4. Resolving a discrepancy records the reviewer, timestamp, and note while preserving the original
   evidence.
5. Disconnecting Xero or QuickBooks preserves historical records and does not block purchasing.

## Domain Model

All new tables are tenant-scoped, carry `tenant_id`, use `ENABLE` and `FORCE` RLS, and pin
cross-tenant foreign keys with `(tenant_id, id)` composite references where the parent supports
them.

### Purchase Order

Stores the internal order reference, supplier, status, currency, ordered total, order date,
expected delivery date, and source/audit metadata. Statuses are `draft`, `submitted`, `confirmed`,
`partially_received`, `received`, `cancelled`, and `closed`.

### Purchase Order Line

Stores the product, description, ordered quantity, unit price, currency, tax treatment, and line
total. Monetary values always carry an explicit currency.

### Supplier Confirmation

Stores a supplier-provided confirmation reference, confirmation date, expected delivery date,
and confirmed quantities/prices per purchase-order line. It may be imported or entered by an
authorized owner or buyer.

### Delivery Receipt

Stores the receipt reference, delivery date, receiving member, and received quantities per
purchase-order line. Partial receipts are first-class records; a line can be received over more
than one delivery.

### Three-Way Match

Stores the current normalized relationship between a purchase order, delivery evidence, and an
accounting bill. It records the comparison result, tolerance ruleset version, matched quantities,
matched amounts, and the source IDs used to derive the result.

### Reconciliation Discrepancy

Extend the existing reconciliation model with three-way discrepancy types without changing the
meaning of existing R3.1 bill/purchase discrepancies. Resolved discrepancies reopen when a
source record changes after resolution, rather than creating duplicate rows.

## Provider Boundary

The accounting connector remains read-only and provider-agnostic. The domain interface exposes
normalized operations such as:

- `build_authorization_url(state)`
- `exchange_code_for_tokens(...)`
- `company_info()`
- `list_bills(since)`
- `list_vendors()`
- `list_purchase_orders(since)` where supported

Xero and QuickBooks identifiers, status names, pagination rules, and response shapes stay inside
their connector implementations. The domain stores `provider` and opaque provider references,
not provider-specific payloads.

If a provider cannot supply purchase orders or delivery receipts, the system must show that the
source is unavailable and continue reconciling the evidence it does have. It must not fabricate a
receipt or treat an absent provider field as zero.

## Matching and Reconciliation

Matching proceeds in stages:

1. Resolve supplier identity using the existing persistent provider-vendor link.
2. Resolve provider invoice to an internal purchase order using provider references first, then
   supplier, currency, amount, product identity, and a bounded date window.
3. Resolve delivery receipts to order lines using order reference, line identity, and explicit
   receiving quantities.
4. Compare ordered, confirmed, received, and invoiced quantities and monetary values.
5. Create or update discrepancy rows with the exact evidence and comparison outcome.

Automatic matching requires one unambiguous candidate. Multiple qualifying candidates remain
unmatched for review. Quantity and price tolerances must be explicit, versioned, and tested;
currency mismatches are never silently converted or accepted.

## Failure and Safety Behavior

- OAuth state is signed, time-limited, and verified with constant-time comparison.
- Access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and only decrypted through the service-role
  path used for provider synchronization.
- Sync attempts use advisory locks so the same connection cannot run two syncs concurrently.
- Provider authentication failures mark the connection `needs_reauth`; they do not erase history.
- Provider rate limits and network failures record a failed/incomplete sync and leave prior data
  visible with its last-synced timestamp.
- No external provider mutation method exists on the connector protocol.
- Cross-tenant reads resolve as not found, and all tenant-scoped writes are database-enforced.
- Every connect, disconnect, sync, import, receipt, match, and discrepancy-resolution action is
  auditable.

## User Interface

Add an order-tracking/reconciliation view linked from existing purchasing surfaces:

- Order header with supplier, order status, expected delivery, and invoice state.
- Line-level comparison of ordered, confirmed, received, and invoiced quantities/prices.
- Clear badges for matched, partially received, over-billed, under-delivered, and unmatched.
- Discrepancy actions for owners/buyers only; viewers can inspect evidence where permitted.
- Explicit last-synced and source labels for provider-derived values.
- English and Arabic translations, RTL-safe layout, keyboard access, and axe-core coverage.

The screen is operational and evidence-led. It is not a dashboard-only surface and every open
exception has a concrete review or resolution action.

## Verification Gates

- Unit tests for Xero parsing, provider normalization, candidate selection, tolerance boundaries,
  and discrepancy reopening.
- Integration tests for RLS, sync retries, advisory-lock exclusivity, provider reauthorization,
  partial receipts, and cross-tenant not-found behavior.
- E2E flow: create/record order -> confirmation -> receipt -> provider bill -> inspect match ->
  resolve discrepancy.
- Accessibility tests in English and Arabic, including keyboard-only resolution.
- Security review covering OAuth state, token encryption, read-only provider calls, rate limits,
  audit events, and tenant isolation.
- G3 evidence must demonstrate integration-sourced data freshness and enough purchase history for
  the next predictive-procurement gate.

## Decisions and Open Boundaries

- Xero is the second provider for R3.3.
- QuickBooks remains supported; the release must not regress R3.1 behavior.
- Supplier confirmations and delivery receipts are internal/imported evidence in this release,
  not a new external supplier portal.
- Provider capability gaps are represented explicitly as unavailable evidence, never inferred as
  successful delivery or matching.
