# Workflow F: campaign review and optimisation

**Lead:** `campaign-review` · **Upstream skills:** `analytics`, `ab-testing`, `cro`, `copy-editing` · **Gates:** none for analysis; changes to live assets go through G-publish or G-live-change

1. Collect real performance data (platform exports, analytics). Record the source and date range. No data: stop and say so.
2. Compare against the objectives and baselines in `measurement-plan.md`.
3. Identify patterns and changes. Keep **measured observations** and **hypotheses** in separate lists.
4. Recommend improvements, each with its evidence and the expected effect stated as a hypothesis.
5. Add experiments to `experiment-backlog.md`: hypothesis, metric, minimum sample or duration, decision rule.
6. Write `performance-report.md` from `analytics/reporting-template.md`.
7. Never fabricate numbers or claim an improvement that was not measured.
