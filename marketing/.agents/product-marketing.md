### ProcurePilot: verified facts (claims approved 2026-10-27; see proof-points for caveats)

- Live at procurepilot.iron-sys.com as an invitation-only pilot, free for the agreed pilot period.
- Reads supplier quotations sent by email or upload; each extracted line links to its source page with a confidence score and is reviewed before it is trusted.
- Compares offers pack for pack with delivery fees included, scoring shown; can split one order across suppliers.
- A person approves every purchase, with the branch's remaining budget shown. No autonomous purchasing.
- A saving counts only once someone verifies its evidence.
- Arabic and English. Product team's target: multi-branch restaurant groups in Egypt, then Saudi Arabia.
- **All figures on screens, the landing page and demo videos are synthetic.** No customers, results or quotes exist.
- Claims register with caveats: `ProcurePilot/knowledge/proof-points.md` (all `proposed`).
