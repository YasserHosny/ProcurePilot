## Layout question: where does the English go?

LinkedIn sets the whole post right to left when it starts in Arabic, so the English paragraphs read awkwardly
(full stops and bullets jump to the wrong side). Options:

1. **Arabic post, English in the first comment** (recommended): the post reads cleanly; English readers still get it.
2. **Two separate posts** (Arabic one day, English another): cleanest, but doubles the posting.
3. **Keep both in one post** and accept the layout.
