# Campaign brief: Iron Systems, "AI that works in production" (first company series)

Plan and decisions: `strategy/company-marketing-plan.md` (CD-01 to CD-04). Claims: `knowledge/company/proof-points.md`
(CO-01 to CO-10, awaiting approval) and, for the proof post, ProcurePilot's approved claims.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Primary objective (one) | Introduce Iron Systems on its new and existing channels as a builder of AI solutions that run in production |
| Secondary objective (optional) | Project inquiries to hello@iron-sys.com; credibility for ProcurePilot (CD-01) |
| Target persona(s) | Owners, operations leaders and IT leads of mid-size businesses in Egypt and Saudi Arabia (hypotheses, company plan) |
| Audience problem | Hours lost on documents, approvals and re-typed data; AI that looks good in a demo but never runs in production |
| Desired outcome for the audience | See that Iron Systems builds AI that is evaluated, keeps people in control, and is already running in its own product |
| Core message (from approved messaging) | Iron Systems builds AI solutions for businesses, designed, built and run in production (CD-02) |
| Primary call to action | Visit www.iron-sys.com or email hello@iron-sys.com with the problem you want to solve (the website's own contact wording) |
| Destination (URL) | https://www.iron-sys.com (UTM per channel once posting starts) |
| Channels (organic only) | Iron Systems LinkedIn page, X, Facebook, Instagram (CD-04) |
| Languages | English and Modern Standard Arabic (CD-03); Arabic written, not translated; Western digits |
| Timing | Four ideas over two weeks after approval; each idea goes out on all four channels the same week |
| Inputs (approved knowledge used) | Website copy (a1904c7), LinkedIn About v1.4, brand v3.3, ProcurePilot claims C-01 to C-17 |
| Constraints | No clients, results, team size, years, certifications or offices. ProcurePilot screens are synthetic data, labelled. Never "the only Arabic…" (D-13). Brand: Iron Dark + one Power Orange accent; no robots, brains, sparkles |
| Success measure | Inquiries and pilot conversations that mention a post (Yasser notes the source); followers and engagement monthly |

- Website changes (articles were not chosen, CD-04).
