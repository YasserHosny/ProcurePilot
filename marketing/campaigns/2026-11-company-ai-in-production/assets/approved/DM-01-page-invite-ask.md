# DM-01: page invite ask

**Why:** members who aren't page admins can invite their own 1st-degree connections to follow a page with fewer
than 5,000 followers: up to 50 a month, or 30 a month for their employer's page. This uses their quota, not the
page's 300 Premium credits. Steps verified against LinkedIn Help "Invite connections to follow a LinkedIn Page you
don't manage" (help/lms/answer/a590020), 2026-11-11.

## v3 (current): English, formal, no how-to

Yasser didn't like v2's invite paragraph (menu path and monthly limits). v3 makes the same ask in one plain
sentence; the "More > Invite connections" steps go in a reply only if someone asks how. He also dropped "and our
LinkedIn page is new", so the follow line names the page itself.

---

Hi [Name],

I hope you are well. We recently launched Iron Systems, which builds AI solutions and software for businesses.

I would be grateful if you could follow our LinkedIn page: https://www.linkedin.com/company/iron-sys

If anyone in your network might find it relevant, I would also appreciate it if you invited them to follow the page.

Thank you for your support.

Best regards,
Yasser
