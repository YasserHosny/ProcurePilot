## Promise

For a purchasing manager at a Saudi restaurant group, this video shows one low-stock report becoming an RFQ to three
suppliers, quotes in riyals read without retyping, a like-for-like comparison, a manager's approval and a sent PO, with
the saving recorded, all in the real product.
