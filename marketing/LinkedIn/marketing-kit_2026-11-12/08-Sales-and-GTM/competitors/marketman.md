## Strengths & Weaknesses

**Strengths**: a mature, well-known category leader with transparent pricing and deep US distributor integrations.
**Weaknesses (for our market)**: no observed Arabic or MENA localisation. Its value depends on EDI with US distributors, which Egyptian suppliers don't have.

## Competitive Implications for ProcurePilot

Unlikely to come up in Egypt-pilot deals. Use it as a **pricing anchor**: back-of-house SaaS for one operator is priced at $249–449 a month in the US. It also shows the "invoice scan → price alerts" pattern, which buyers may confuse with quote comparison. Explain the difference: an invoice tells you what you already paid; a quote tells you what you could pay.
