## Positioning & Messaging

**Primary value proposition**: "Powering smarter procurement, inventory management, and data-driven decision making."

**Target audience**: multi-branch F&B groups (fine dining, QSR, casual dining, cafés and roasteries, cloud kitchens, bars). The named customers are mostly GCC brands, e.g. MAMA'ESH (22 locations), La Carnita, Tashas, and Donk Group ("10 locations for Riyadh Season").

**Positioning angle**: an all-in-one back-of-house system with control and permissions. The pitch is "200+ customisable user permissions, ordering policies and approval workflows".

**Key messaging themes**:
- Lower food cost: "25% food cost reduction", and a quote about food cost dropping "from 39% to 33%" (homepage)
- Controls and approvals to prevent overspend (homepage)
- AI: predictive ordering, AI invoice receiving, AI sales forecasting, and "Anomaly detection AI (coming soon)" (nav)

## Product & Features

- **Procurement:** requisitions, approvals, POs, central kitchen ordering and price-variance monitoring, on mobile or the web portal
- **Supplier management:** POs sent "via email, WhatsApp, or direct integrations", plus delivery schedules, credit terms and contracts, and "track pricing across countries and currencies". Supplier prices are maintained by bulk edit or by Excel export and re-import.
- **Inventory:** recipes, allergens, counts, wastage, transfers and audit logs
- **BI:** dashboards, spreadsheet reports, open API and Delta Sharing
- **Supy Connect:** a B2B ordering app for central kitchens that sell to outside customers, so the supply side runs on Supy too
- **Integrations:** "50+ live POS integrations", "Over 75 integrations", covering accounting, ERP and delivery aggregators
- Supy publishes comparison pages against Apicbase, CrunchTime, MarketMan, Nory, **Odoo**, Oracle Simphony, Restaurant365, Restoke and **spreadsheets**

**Not observed on the pages checked (2026-10-28):** RFQs, collecting competing quotes from several suppliers, extracting prices from supplier PDFs or photos, or side-by-side quote comparison. AI invoice reading exists, but it works on invoices, not quotes. Check this in a demo before claiming it publicly.

## Strengths & Weaknesses

**Strengths**
- The closest regional match to our ICP: multi-branch F&B, Arabic site, GCC customers, WhatsApp PO delivery (observed)
- Broad product with deep POS and accounting integrations (observed claims)
- Low starting price for what it covers (observed)

**Weaknesses (inferred, medium confidence)**
- The price workflow assumes a known price list per supplier (bulk edit, Excel import). Comparing competing quotes doesn't appear on the pages checked.
- Its proof is food-cost percentage and inventory variance, not purchasing savings per sourcing event.

## Competitive Implications for ProcurePilot

- **Where they're strong vs. us:** inventory, recipes and COGS; POS breadth; GCC references; an established implementation team.
- **Where we may be strong (hypotheses until pilot evidence):** turning messy quotes (PDF, photo, email, Arabic) into a comparable basket; RFQs; splitting a basket across suppliers; savings proven per decision.
- **Opportunity:** position *alongside* Supy, not against it. Supy runs stock and POs; ProcurePilot decides *who to buy from at what price*. Ask prospects who already use Supy how they pick suppliers today.
- **Threat:** Supy already has the approvals, permissions and WhatsApp PO plumbing. Adding a quote-comparison module would be a short step for them.
