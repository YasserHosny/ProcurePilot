## ProcurePilot in one paragraph

ProcurePilot is **purchasing software built in Arabic**. It is for multi-branch restaurant groups in **Egypt and
Saudi Arabia** whose suppliers send quotes in Arabic, as PDFs, photos and WhatsApp messages. It reads those quotes,
compares them on their true landed cost (pack for pack, delivery fees included), can split one order across
suppliers when that costs less, and counts a saving only once someone on the team verifies it. A person approves
every purchase, from the web or the phone. It replaces WhatsApp threads and Excel sheets. Status: **live as an
invitation-only pilot, free for the agreed pilot period**. Built by Iron Systems.

- Headline 1: *The cheapest quote isn't always the cheapest order.* «أرخص عرض سعر مش دايماً أرخص طلبية»
- Headline 2: *Did you really save money, or do you just think you did?* «وفّرت فعلاً.. ولا فاكر إنك وفّرت؟»
- Call to action: *Talk to us about a pilot* · hello@iron-sys.com · procurepilot.iron-sys.com

## Rules (from the approved claims register)

**Use**
- Only the approved claims in `01-Idea-and-Positioning/proof-points.md` (C-01 to C-17, except C-13 which is held),
  with the limits written next to each.
- Label every product screen or demo figure: **"Real screens with synthetic data."** All suppliers, prices and
  savings in screens and videos are demo data («مطاعم سند» Egypt, «مطاعم رواق» Saudi Arabia).
- "Built in Arabic", "invitation-only pilot, free for the agreed pilot period", "Built by Iron Systems".
- When mentioning WhatsApp, keep the limitation in the same sentence: suppliers keep sending on WhatsApp; you save the
  file and upload it or forward it by email. There's no direct WhatsApp connection.

**Never**
- Savings percentages, money saved, time saved, ROI. No real customer figure exists yet.
- Customer names, logos, testimonials, case studies. There are no customers on record yet.
- Extraction accuracy figures (not measured).
- ERP / Odoo integration, push notifications or real-time alerts, "secure", "bank-grade", "certified", "compliant".
- "The only Arabic…", or comparisons naming a competitor in public.
- Pilot length or future price (internal).

**Brand**
- Product brand: Converge logo, Navy `#0B2545` + Teal `#12A594`, Readex Pro (see `03-…/README.md`). Never recolour
  the bars or mirror the mark in Arabic layouts.
- Company brand: Iron Systems identity v3.3, Iron Dark + Power Orange (see `02-…/brand-identity.md`).
- Arabic copy: Egyptian dialect for Egypt, Gulf for Saudi Arabia, with a native check before publishing.
