## About (live, English)

```
Iron Systems builds AI solutions for businesses. We design, build and run AI that solves real business problems, from the first idea to a system working in production, built on your own data and with checks in place.

What we build
• AI assistants and copilots that work inside your tools and understand your business.
• Document AI that reads, classifies and checks contracts, invoices, emails and forms.
• Knowledge search: answers from your own content, with sources you can check.
• Intelligent automation: AI agents that handle routine decisions and pass on the exceptions.
• Predictive analytics: forecasts and scores measured against real outcomes.
• AI integration: secure connections between models and the systems you already use (ERP, CRM, data pipelines), plus the custom software around them.

How we work
Problem first: we understand the work before we choose the technology.
AI where it helps: we test whether a model adds value before we ship it.
Built to last: clean, documented systems your team can own.

Our product
ProcurePilot is our own AI copilot for procurement, from request to approved purchase. It reads requests written in plain language, compares supplier quotes, flags issues before approval and keeps an audit trail of every decision. It is live in an invitation-only pilot: https://procurepilot.iron-sys.com/
```
