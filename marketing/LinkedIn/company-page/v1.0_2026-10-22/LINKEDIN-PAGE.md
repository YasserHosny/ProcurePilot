## Step by step

1. Sign in to LinkedIn with your personal account (it must be in good standing; new accounts with very few connections are sometimes blocked from creating pages).
2. Go to **linkedin.com/company/setup/new** (or **For Business** (grid icon) → **Create a Company Page** → **Company**).
3. Fill the form with the **Page identity** values below. For the public URL try `iron-systems`; if it is taken, use `iron-sys` (matches your domain) or `ironsystems`.
4. Upload the logo, enter the tagline, tick the box that you are an authorised representative, and click **Create page**.
5. On the new page, click **Edit page** and fill **Description**, **Specialties**, location and contact details from the sections below.
6. Click the camera on the banner and upload the cover image.
7. Optional, for Arabic: **Edit page → Page info → Manage languages → Add language → Arabic**, then paste the Arabic name, tagline and description. Arabic-language members then see the Arabic version.
8. Set the **custom button** (Edit page → Buttons) to **Visit website**, pointing to the website URL.
9. Add yourself (and anyone else) as **Super admin** under **Admin tools → Manage admins** so the page is not tied to one person only.
10. Ask the team to add Iron Systems as their current employer, so they appear under People. Then publish a first post (suggestion at the bottom).
