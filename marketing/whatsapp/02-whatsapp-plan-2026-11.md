## 1. Goal and how we measure it

| | Target by 2026-12-31 | Why this number |
|---|---|---|
| Founders contacted one to one | 80 (about 20 a week from week 2) | What one person can do well alongside the rest of the work, and stays under WhatsApp's spam limits |
| Replies | 20 or more (25%) | A warm, personal first message with a question should get one in four. If we're under 15% after 40 sends, the opener changes |
| Calls or demos held | 8 | Roughly 2 in 5 who reply and see the problem |
| Pilots agreed | 2 or 3 | The first-customer goal in the outreach plan |

These are working targets, not forecasts. We don't have results yet; after the first 40 sends we reset them
on what really happened.

**The main measure is conversations**, not views or followers (CD-06).

## 2. Who we contact

Founders, owners and general managers of companies in Egypt that **buy from many suppliers across several
branches** (MD-20). The person who feels the pain and can say yes to a pilot.

| Priority | Sector | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Restaurant and café groups (3 or more branches) | Our demo is built on them; quotes arrive on WhatsApp every day |
| 2 | Clinic, lab and pharmacy chains | Many suppliers, packs and units that are hard to compare |
| 3 | Food retail, bakeries, catering, cloud kitchens | Daily buying, delivery charges vary |
| 4 | Contractors and facility-management companies | Large quotes, VAT and delivery matter |

Skip: one-branch businesses, companies with a full ERP purchasing team (not our first customer), anyone who
asked not to be contacted.

## 3. Where the numbers come from (in order of quality)

| Source | How | Allowed first message |
|---|---|---|
| **Introductions** | Ask friends, family, ex-colleagues: "who do you know who owns a business with several branches?" (kit message 10) | Opener B4, mention who introduced you |
| **Events and meetups** | Swap numbers in person (outreach plan, Events) | Opener B1, mention where you met |
| **LinkedIn conversations** | After a LinkedIn reply, ask "is WhatsApp easier for you?" | Opener B1 |
| **Groups you joined** | Founders who reply to your group post or question post | Follow up privately on what they wrote |
| **Published business numbers** | The number on the company's own website, Google Maps or Facebook page, published for business contact | Opener B1 or B2, ask for the owner or purchasing person |

**Never**: bought or scraped lists, numbers from a leaked file, adding people to a group or broadcast list
without their OK, a personal number nobody gave you.

## 4. Rules that protect the number and the brand

1. **First message: text only, no image, no link, no PDF.** Ask a question. Images go only after they reply.
2. **Make saying no easy.** Every follow-up offers "just tell me and I won't message again".
3. **Two messages at most without a reply** (opener plus one follow-up after 3 days). Then stop.
4. **No more than 20 new chats a day**, spread over the day. New WhatsApp Business numbers get flagged when
   many people block or report them.
5. **Broadcast lists only for people who've replied** and agreed to hear updates. WhatsApp only delivers a
   broadcast to people who saved your number anyway.
6. **Prices only one to one, after a call** (MD-07). Never in a group, on Status or in an image.
7. **Nothing we can't prove.** No customers, savings figures or accuracy numbers until they are real. Every
   number in our images is marked "illustrative example".
8. **Keep a record** of who agreed to be contacted and who said no (Egypt Law 151/2020, arts 17 and 18;
   lawyer to confirm, still open).

## 6. The conversation path

| Step | When | What to send | Image |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Opener | Day 0 | Founder opener B1 to B4 (`01-group-messages.md`) | none |
| 2. Follow-up | Day 3, if no reply | B5, then stop | none |
| 3. They reply: show the problem | Same day | Reflect what they said, then C1 or C2 | `1-whatsapp-chaos-to-decision` or `3-hidden-costs` |
| 4. Interest: how it works | Same chat | "It's 3 steps, you don't change how you work" | `2-three-steps`, then the 1-minute Arabic demo video |
| 5. Worry about control or trust | When it comes up | "The system prepares, you decide" | `4-you-stay-in-control` |
| 6. Book the call | Same chat | "15 minutes this week? I'll show it on an example like yours" | none |
| 7. After the call | Same day | Kit message 11 plus the one-pager PDF | `5-founding-pilot-invite` |
| 8. Offer | Only after a good call | Kit message 12, the private offer PDF | none (the PDF has the terms) |
| 9. Not now | Whenever | Thank them, label "Not now", one check-in after 6 weeks | none |

Never send more than two images in a row. One image plus one question works better than a full gallery.

## 7. Groups and Status (supporting the one-to-one work)

- **Groups**: join 5 to 8 groups where Egyptian founders, restaurant owners, clinic or pharmacy owners and
  purchasing people talk. Week 1: read and help, don't post. Week 2: the question post (A3). Week 3 or later:
  the main post (A1) with image 1, only where the admin agreed. One post per group per month at most.
- **Status**: twice a week, rotating the five images and the three Status images in `images/status/`, plus the
  vertical demo video once a week. People who saved the number see it, which keeps quiet contacts warm.
