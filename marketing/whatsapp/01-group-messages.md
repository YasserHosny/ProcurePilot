# Group posts, founder openers and offer messages

Product first, by Iron Systems. Drafts to change and send yourself. Arabic first, English if they write in English.

**The image:** `images/send/offer-ar.png` and `images/send/offer-en.png` (1080×1350). It shows an illustrative example: the lowest quote isn't the cheapest order once pack size and delivery are counted. No real prices, customers or savings.

## Rules

- Groups you don't own: ask the admin before posting. One post per group, then answer replies.
- Founders: the **first message has no image or link**. Ask first; send the image only after they answer.
- Never add anyone to a group without asking.
- Our own prices stay private: only in a one-to-one chat, after a call.
- Still true today: no customers, no savings figure, no start date. Don't promise any of them.

## B. Opening messages to founders (one to one)

Short, about their business, ending with an easy question. No image or link in the first message.

### B1. You know their company

> **AR:** أهلًا [الاسم]، شايف إن [اسم الشركة] عندها [عدد] فروع، ما شاء الله. سؤال سريع: المشتريات عندكم مركزية ولا كل فرع بيشتري لوحده؟ بسأل عشان شغالين على نظام بيسهّل مقارنة عروض الموردين، ومحتاج أفهم الناس بتشتغل إزاي.
>
> **EN:** Hi [name], I see [company] has [number] branches, well done. Quick question: is purchasing central, or does each branch buy on its own? I'm asking because we're building a system that makes comparing supplier quotes easier, and I want to understand how people really work.

### B2. Shorter, straight to the pain

> **AR:** أهلًا [الاسم]، سؤال واحد بس: لما بيجيلكم عرضين من موردين مختلفين بعبوات مختلفة، بتعرفوا بسهولة مين الأوفر فعلًا؟
>
> **EN:** Hi [name], just one question: when two suppliers quote with different pack sizes, can you easily tell which one is really cheaper?

### B3. Founder to founder

> **AR:** أهلًا [الاسم]، من مؤسس لمؤسس: إحنا في Iron Systems بنبني ProcurePilot للمشتريات، ومش جاي أبيع، عايز رأيك. هل مقارنة عروض الموردين مشكلة حقيقية عندكم ولا لا؟ ردك هيفرق معانا جدًا.
>
> **EN:** Hi [name], founder to founder: at Iron Systems we're building ProcurePilot for purchasing, and I'm not here to sell, I'd like your view. Is comparing supplier quotes a real problem for you or not? Your answer would help us a lot.

### B4. Someone introduced you

> **AR:** أهلًا [الاسم]، [اسم اللي عرّفنا] قالي إنك ممكن تفيدني. بنعمل ProcurePilot للشركات اللي بتشتري من موردين كتير، وحابب أسمع منك عن طريقة شغلكم في المشتريات. ينفع ١٠ دقايق في وقت يناسبك؟
>
> **EN:** Hi [name], [who introduced us] said you might help me. We're building ProcurePilot for companies buying from many suppliers, and I'd like to hear how you handle purchasing. Could we have 10 minutes whenever suits you?

### B5. Follow-up, once only, after 3 days

> **AR:** أهلًا [الاسم]، عارف إنك مشغول. لو الموضوع مش في أولوياتك دلوقتي قولي بس وأنا مش هزعجك تاني 🙏
>
> **EN:** Hi [name], I know you're busy. If this isn't a priority right now, just say so and I won't bother you again 🙏

## C. Offer messages (after they answer, with the image)

### C1. They said it's a real problem

Send `offer-ar.png`, then:

> **AR:** الصورة دي بتوضح الفكرة: أقل سعر مش دايمًا أرخص طلبية لما تحسب العبوة والتوصيل. ProcurePilot بيعمل الحساب ده لوحده، من عروض الواتساب والصور وPDF، وحد من فريقك هو اللي يوافق في الآخر. بنعمل تجربة مجانية مع أول شركات وبنجهّزها معاكم. تحب أوريك في ربع ساعة؟
>
> **EN:** This image shows the idea: the lowest price isn't always the cheapest order once pack size and delivery count. ProcurePilot does that sum for you, from WhatsApp quotes, photos and PDFs, and someone on your team approves at the end. We're running free pilots with our first companies and setting them up with you. Can I show you in 15 minutes?

### C2. They asked "what is it?"

> **AR:** ProcurePilot نظام مشتريات من Iron Systems. بتبعتله عروض الموردين زي ما هي، وهو يقراها ويقارنها صح ويقولك مين الأوفر فعلًا، وإنت اللي بتوافق. الصورة فيها مثال. التجربة مجانية، تحب نبدأ بمكالمة قصيرة؟
>
> **EN:** ProcurePilot is a purchasing system by Iron Systems. You send it supplier quotes as they are; it reads them, compares them properly and shows which is really cheaper, and you approve. The image has an example. The pilot is free. Shall we start with a short call?

### C3. They're interested but busy

> **AR:** ولا يهمك. هبعتلك الصورة والموقع تبص عليهم وقت ما تفضى: https://procurepilot.iron-sys.com . لما يبقى عندك ربع ساعة قولي ونحدد.
>
> **EN:** No problem. Here's the image and the site to look at when you're free: https://procurepilot.iron-sys.com . When you have 15 minutes, tell me and we'll set a time.
