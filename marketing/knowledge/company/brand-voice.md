# Iron Systems: brand voice

Built from copy that is already live (website `lib/i18n/en.ts`, LinkedIn page v1.2) and the brand's own writing
rules, not from adjectives picked in a meeting. The attributes are an interpretation of that copy; the example lines
are verbatim.

## Voice attributes

| Attribute | Means | Doesn't mean | Real line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plain** | Short sentences, everyday words, the concrete thing first | Dumbed down, chatty, slang | "Describe the problem you want to solve. A few sentences is enough to start the conversation." |
| **Specific** | Name the document, the step, the system | Feature lists without context, vague "solutions" talk | "Data is re-typed between ERP, CRM, accounting and spreadsheets, and the copies drift apart." |
| **Calm and confident** | State what we do; let the work carry weight | Hype, superlatives, exclamation marks, urgency tricks | "AI that works in production." |
| **Engineering-honest** | Say how it is checked, where people stay in control, what is sample data | Overclaiming, hiding limits, "fully autonomous" promises | "Workflows that handle the routine and route exceptions to a person, with every step visible and open to override." |
| **Problem first** | Open with the reader's work and pain, then what we build | Opening with technology or with ourselves | "Hours lost reading documents, answering repeat questions and making calls on incomplete data." |

## Tone by context

| Context | Shift |
|---|---|
| Website, product pages | Most formal. Problem → what we build → how it is checked. One action. |
| LinkedIn thought leadership | First-person plural ("we"), opinionated about engineering practice, still evidence-led. |
| LinkedIn / Instagram / Facebook product education | Show the real screen, explain one step, label synthetic data. |
| Announcements | State what changed, who it is for, how to get access. No "revolutionary". |
| Arabic | Written for Arabic readers, not translated word for word. Website uses Modern Standard Arabic. Dialect for social or video is a per-campaign decision (precedents: Egyptian in demo v2.2, Gulf in v2.3). |

## Words and patterns

**Use:** build, design, run, in production, your own data, evaluated, monitored, a person approves, every step
visible, real screens, synthetic data (when it is), problem first.

**Avoid (and why):**

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, next-gen, magic | Hype; the brand is calm and precise |
| fully autonomous, replaces your team, no humans needed | Contradicts the human-in-the-loop principle and the product facts |
| "trusted by", "leading", "#1", "industry-leading" | No evidence exists yet |
| invented percentages, savings, hours saved | Brand rule: never invent metrics or results |
| robots, brains, sparkles (in visuals or emoji) | Brand imagery rule |
| "Iron System", "IronSystems", "Iron-Sys" in running copy | Name is **Iron Systems** |
| "Procure Pilot", "Procurepilot" | Product name is **ProcurePilot** |
