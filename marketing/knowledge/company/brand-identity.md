# Iron Systems: brand identity (marketing summary)

This is a working summary for marketing. The authority is `<workspace>/Branding/Identity-v3/README.md`, the full
guidelines `<workspace>/Branding/Identity-v3/index.html` and the PDF `<workspace>/Branding/Iron Systems Brand Guidelines v3.3.pdf`.
If this file and the brand folder ever disagree, the brand folder wins; fix this file.

**Never** use `<workspace>/Branding/_archive/` (v2 "Iron System", I+S mark, copper palette, Manrope).

## Concept

- **Iron** = structure: the custom software and engineering side.
- **Heat** (Power Orange) = intelligence: the AI, "the active, intelligent part of every system".
- **System** = one hexagon and one 30° angle: "a system, not a collection of tools".
- Personality: **strong, precise, calm, premium**. Dark and metallic with one hot accent. Never playful, never neon, never "generic AI" blue-purple.
- Reference words for briefing writers, designers and motion artists: Forged · Precise · Powered · Load-bearing · Calm · Engineered.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Iron Dark | `#0B0F17` | Primary background; text on orange |
| Iron 2 | `#111827` | Cards, panels |
| Iron 3 | `#1E293B` | Borders and graphics on dark |
| Steel Gray | `#475569` | Secondary text on light |
| Steel 4 | `#94A3B8` | Secondary text on dark |
| Iron Light | `#E2E8F0` | Text and logo on dark |
| White | `#F8FAFC` | Light background |
| Power Orange | `#F97316` | The one accent: shard, tip, rules, primary button |
| Orange Deep | `#C2410C` | Orange text on light backgrounds |

Rules: Iron Dark and Iron 2 carry about 75% of a layout. Orange marks **one thing per view**. **Never white text on
orange** (buttons use Iron Dark text). Dark is the default theme everywhere; light only when chosen.

## Type

- **Archivo Expanded** (500–800): wordmark, display lines, short headings; caps for display lines.
- **Inter** (300–700): everything else.
- **Readex Pro** for Arabic: no letter-spacing, taller line heights. Keep "Iron Systems", product names and acronyms in Latin script; Western digits.
- Orange in type only on the final word of a statement.

## Logo

- Flat master by default (UI, documents, social templates, small sizes). Metal finish only for heroes, covers, launch posts, signage, video end frames; once per layout; at least 120 px tall.
- **No glow, halo or lens flare** on the metal mark (removed in v3.2).
- Never redraw, retype, stretch, rotate, outline, recolour, mirror or translate the logo.
- Files by need: see the "Which file to use" table in `<workspace>/Branding/Identity-v3/README.md`. Most used for marketing:
  - Social avatar: `logo/png/iron-system-app-icon-metal-1024.png`
  - OG / link preview: `logo/png/iron-systems-og-1200x630.png`
  - Slides, video end frames: `logo/png/iron-systems-cover-1920x1080.png` plus metal SVGs
  - Hero and cover background: `imagery/hero-bg.jpg` (dark), `imagery/hero-bg-light.jpg`
  - Canva or other tools without SVG: `logo/png/`

## Layout and graphic elements

- 12-column grid, one left edge, headlines span 7 columns. Square corners; one 30° slant per object, bottom right.
- Elements (use one or two per layout): 3 px orange rule, //// slashes at 30°, angled corner, dot grid, angled orange plate, hex lattice (never behind text), mark crop (bleeds off an edge), heat edge.
- Icons: 24 px grid, 2 px stroke, one orange "core" detail. **No robots, brains, sparkles or circuit traces.**

## Imagery

- Do: metal macro, real engineers at work (unposed), the split composition (raw material | clean software, split by an orange shard on the 30° lean). Grade toward Iron Dark.
- Don't: blue-purple AI gradients, neon, glowing circuits, generic 3D robots, brains, chips, holograms, factories or welders, stock AI imagery.

## Product sub-brands

Products may have their own visual identity. ProcurePilot has one (Navy `#0B2545`, Teal `#12A594`, Readex Pro, the
"Converge" logo; see `ProcurePilot/design-system/`). Rules:

- Product materials use the product's identity; company materials use Iron Systems'.
- Product material closes with Iron Systems as the maker where appropriate (precedent: "Developed by Iron Systems" end card in the ProcurePilot demo video v2.3).
- Never mix the two palettes in one composition except in an explicit maker/endorsement frame.

## Do and don't (verbatim rules from the brand)

- Do let Iron Dark lead and use orange for one thing. Don't fill layouts with orange.
- Do use the flat logo by default. Don't invent chrome, bevels or neon glow.
- Do show real work and real engineers. Don't use stock AI imagery.
- **Do write plain, specific copy about what we build. Don't invent clients, testimonials, metrics or results.**
