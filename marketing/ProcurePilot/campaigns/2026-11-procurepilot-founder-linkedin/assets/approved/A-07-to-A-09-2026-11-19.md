## A-07 · Split one order across suppliers

```
The supplier with the best price on one item isn't always the cheapest on all of them. So why send the whole order to one supplier?

ProcurePilot compares offers pack for pack, with each supplier's delivery fee included. When splitting the order across suppliers lowers the total, it tells you and shows the numbers. A person on your team still decides, and nothing is ordered before they approve.

Do you split orders across suppliers, or buy everything from one?

procurepilot.iron-sys.com
```

## A-08 · One quote request to every supplier

```
Getting prices from six suppliers means six messages, six replies, and a spreadsheet you fill in by hand.

In ProcurePilot you create one request for quotation and send it to every supplier at once, in Arabic or English. Sending is always your action. Suppliers reply by email, or you upload their quote, and the offers are read and compared side by side.

How do you ask suppliers for quotes today?

procurepilot.iron-sys.com
```

## A-09 · One assistant for the whole purchasing cycle

```
Every company that buys goes through the same steps, whatever its field: someone asks, you collect supplier quotes, compare them, someone approves, then you buy.

ProcurePilot helps across that whole cycle. Send one request for quotation to every supplier. It reads the quotes you get and compares them pack for pack. Each request reaches the approver with the remaining budget shown, and nothing is ordered until someone on your team approves.

It's live as an invitation-only pilot, free for the pilot period. Which purchasing step takes your team the longest?

procurepilot.iron-sys.com
```
