# Campaign brief: founder LinkedIn, first six ProcurePilot posts

**Reference first.** This campaign supports the product team's agreed plan (founder-led sales, $0, one named pilot
in Egypt): `../docs/gtm/first-customer-gtm-plan.md`. Posts give the founder's outreach
something credible to point to (MD-01); they do not replace it. Every line reuses approved wording: the approved
positioning, claims C-01 to C-12, C-14, C-15, and the owner-approved v2.5 video script (SRC-029).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Primary objective (one) | Credibility for the founder's outreach: when a prospect gets Yasser's message or video and checks his profile, they find clear, honest posts about the problem and the product |
| Secondary objective (optional) | Inbound pilot conversations from multi-branch restaurant owners and purchasing managers in Egypt and Saudi Arabia |
| Target persona(s) | Owner / GM, purchasing or operations manager of a 2+ branch restaurant group (`ProcurePilot/knowledge/target-audience.md`, hypotheses) |
| Audience problem | Quotes arrive in every format, prices are retyped into Excel, packs don't match, and nobody knows what was really saved (pain lines from the approved video script) |
| Desired outcome for the audience | See that a fair comparison and a verified saving are possible without changing how suppliers send quotes |
| Core message (from approved messaging) | Headlines MD-09: "The cheapest quote isn't always the cheapest order." · "Did you really save money, or do you just think you did?" Positioning: built in Arabic, verified savings (MD-05, MD-11) |
| Primary call to action | A conversation (MD-04): comment or message Yasser, or email hello@iron-sys.com, with the number of branches and how suppliers send quotes. No start dates, no prices (MD-07) |
| Destination (URL) | None required. procurepilot.iron-sys.com may be linked in English/Egypt posts; not in Saudi posts until the landing page serves Saudi readers (D-11). Hosted runs on staging (D-12): fine for a look, not for real data |
| Channels (organic only) | LinkedIn, Yasser's personal profile (MD-03); the Iron Systems page reshares |
| Languages | Arabic per market: Egyptian for Egypt, Gulf (neutral Saudi) for Saudi Arabia, matching the approved video script; English version of each post for mixed audiences. Western digits in post text (marketing-config Arabic rules) |
| Timing | Six posts over three weeks from approval, two a week (see `content-calendar.md`) |
| Inputs (approved knowledge used) | `ProcurePilot/knowledge/proof-points.md`, `ProcurePilot/knowledge/messaging-matrix.md`, `ProcurePilot/planning/decisions.md`, `research/market-research/2026-10-28-procurepilot-alternatives-egypt-ksa.md` |
| Constraints | Demo data only, always labelled. Never name competitors or claim "the only Arabic…" (D-13). No savings figures, customer names or results. No price. No push-notification or app-store claims (C-09 caveat). WhatsApp: "save and upload or forward", never a direct connection (C-10) |
| Success measure | Qualitative for now: number of pilot conversations that mention a post (Yasser notes them in the SP pipeline). See `measurement-plan.md` |
