# What each person asks, and the honest answer

Several people sign off on a purchase. These are likely questions, written before any real conversation (label H),
so replace them with what people actually ask. Don't go beyond these answers: there are no customers, savings figures
or accuracy figures to quote yet.

## Owner or general manager

| They ask | Answer |
|---|---|
| How much will this save me? | I don't have a real number yet, and I won't invent one. In the pilot we record your prices first, then measure against them. |
| Who are your customers? | You'd be one of the first. That's why the pilot is free and I set it up with you. |
| What does the pilot cost? | Nothing during the agreed pilot period. |
| Could it buy something by mistake? | No. A person on your team approves every purchase. Nothing is ordered before that approval. |
| What happens if we stop? | You get your data as an export, then we delete it. |

## Operations or purchasing manager

| They ask | Answer |
|---|---|
| Our suppliers only use WhatsApp. | They can stay on it. Save the PDF or photo and upload it, or forward it by email. There's no direct WhatsApp connection. |
| What if it reads a quote wrong? | Every line links back to its page in the quote and is reviewed before it's trusted. I can't give you an accuracy figure because it hasn't been measured. |
| Will this replace my job? | No. It prepares the comparison; you and your manager decide. |
| Pack sizes differ between suppliers. | It compares pack for pack with each supplier's delivery fee included. |
| We already order on Suplyd. | Keep it. Put its price next to your other suppliers' before you order. |
| We don't buy food. | The demo shows a restaurant, but the steps are the same. Tell me what you buy and I'll say honestly whether it fits. |

## Finance

| They ask | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can it control branch spending? | Branch requests reach the approver with the remaining budget shown. |
| How do you count a saving? | Only once someone verifies its evidence. |
| Does it connect to our accounting system? | Not in Egypt today. Data exports as CSV for your accountant. |
| Which currency? | Egyptian pounds and Saudi riyals, one currency per comparison, with no exchange rates. |

## IT, if there is any

| They ask | Answer |
|---|---|
| Do you train AI on our data? | No. We don't use your data to train AI models. |
| Is it certified or audited? | No external audit or certification yet. |
| Does it connect to our ERP? | Not today. Tell me which system you use; it helps us plan. |
| Where will our data go? | Your real data goes in only after the separate pilot environment is ready and the agreement is signed. The demo uses synthetic data. |
