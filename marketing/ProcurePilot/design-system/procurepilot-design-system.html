Design system · reference for the next marketing video

How ProcurePilot looks, sounds and speaks

Pulled from the ProcurePilot repo (docs/brand/, the Angular and Flutter app code, docs/gtm/video/) and from the Demo generation session's plan. Every colour below is the exact hex in the source files.

The one thing to know first: ProcurePilot has two visual layers that don't match yet.
  • Brand layer (logo, sting, end cards, one-pager): Navy #0B2545 + Teal #12A594, Readex Pro, the "Converge" logo. Adopted 2026-10-14.
  • Product UI (what the screen recordings show): Angular Material's stock indigo-pink theme, indigo #4F46E5, Roboto, slate greys. The app header still shows a Material "insights" icon next to the word ProcurePilot, not the Converge logo. The phone app is unthemed Flutter Material 3 (lavender).
  • For a marketing video: build every graphic, title and card from the brand layer, and treat app footage as footage. That's what the last videos did.
01 · Logo

Converge

Three bars are supplier quotes compared side by side. The teal one, the best quote, keeps going as an arrow: the decision moving forward. It replaced the old paper-plane mark, which read as Telegram's logo next to WhatsApp screenshots.

Wordmark: Readex Pro 600, tracking −2%, "Procure" in navy and "Pilot" in teal. Arabic lockup «بروكيور بايلوت» puts the mark at the reading start (right). All SVG lettering is outlined.

Logo rules

  • Never mirror the mark in Arabic layouts: the arrow means "forward", not reading direction.
  • Clear space: at least one bar's height (1/8 of the mark) on every side.
  • Below 24 px, use the mark or app icon alone.
  • Don't recolour the bars, outline the mark or add effects.

Patterns to know

  • White top bar: hamburger, "ProcurePilot" + indigo icon, workspace chip, avatar.
  • Right-side nav in Arabic; active item indigo on indigo tint.
  • Page = big bold title, one-line slate subtitle, outlined action buttons.

05 · Motion & sound

The signature moments

Logo sting (approved 2026-10-15)

Grey bars slide in, the teal bar grows past them, the arrowhead pops with one small overshoot and a two-ghost speed trail, then the name is pushed in left to right by the arrow.

Beat-locked to the track: tick, tick, rising swish, pop on beat 3 (1.602 s), soft whoosh, chime on beat 5 (the brand sound). 4.5 s cycle; 3.0 s fast version for vertical cuts.

End card

Navy ground, elements fade up 0.27 s apart: logo · «جرّبه أسبوعين مجانًا» · «وإحنا اللي هنجهّزه معاك.» · WhatsApp +20 102 735 4140 in large teal · «د. ياسر حسني — المؤسس» · yasser.hosny@iron-sys.com · «أحد منتجات Iron Systems».

English: Try it free for 2 weeks · "we set it up with you." · Dr. Yasser Hosny, Founder · A product of Iron Systems. Vertical card keeps clear of the top ~250 px and bottom ~350 px.

Audio

Music «Antalya Beaches» by DayFox (Pixabay, soft house, 112.35 BPM; YouTube Content ID may auto-claim). Music −4 dB under cues; final ≈ −14 LUFS, peaks ≤ −1.6 dBFS. No voice-over: text + music, because social autoplays muted.

06 · Tone

How it speaks

جرّبه أسبوعين مجانًا — وإحنا اللي هنجهّزه معاك.

  • Egyptian Arabic for marketing lines, short and spoken. Arabic first; English is a separate render, never a second line under the Arabic.
  • Concrete restaurant world: oil tins, rice sacks, onions, Maadi branch, WhatsApp quotes, Excel.
  • The promise is money: show the saving in ج.م, counted up.

Honesty rules (from the plan)

  • RFQs are sent by a person. The guardrail drafts a purchase request; the phone approves the request; the PO is made on web.
  • Every number on screen comes from the demo tenant's real savings report.
  • Captions disclose «بيانات تجريبية» / "Demo data". The owner's email is masked in all footage.
  • No fake push notifications: a text card «وصل إشعار للمدير» stands in.
