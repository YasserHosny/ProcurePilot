# ProcurePilot team brief

A standalone PDF that explains the ProcurePilot idea and product to the marketing team, for people who don't read the
Markdown knowledge files. One file per language, same content.

| File | Language | Pages |
|---|---|---|
| `ProcurePilot-Idea-and-Product_EN_2026-11-12.pdf` | English | 10 |
| `ProcurePilot-Idea-and-Product_AR_2026-11-12.pdf` | Arabic (Modern Standard Arabic, right to left) | 10 |

Contents: the problem, the idea and the approved positioning, how it works in seven steps (real screens with
synthetic data), where the product stands, the approved claims and the never/always rules, messaging, brand, and
what other material to ask for.

Sources: `../knowledge/` (claims register, positioning, messaging, target audience), the approved LinkedIn campaign
images and the v2.5 demo-video stills. The Arabic claim wording is a translation for understanding; the English
claims register is the approved wording, and published Arabic copy still needs a native check per market.
