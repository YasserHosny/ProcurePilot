## Message by funnel stage

| Stage | Reader's question | Message | Content formats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unaware / problem-aware | "Is this a real problem?" | Comparing quotes across pack sizes and delivery fees by hand hides the real price | Short educational posts, carousel (pack-for-pack example with synthetic data) |
| Solution-aware | "How do others solve it?" | A quote is only useful if every line can be traced and compared on landed cost | Explainer video, website article |
| Product-aware | "Why this one?" | Traceable, comparable, a person approves, savings verified | Demo video (v2.5 cuts), landing page |
| Ready to act | "How do I start?" | Invitation-only pilot, free for the agreed period; tell us your branches and how suppliers send quotes | Landing pilot section, email to hello@iron-sys.com |

| Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| quote, quotation, supplier, branch, landed cost, pack for pack, delivery fee | "AI copilot", "plain-language requests" | Matches the product's own copy; the LinkedIn wording isn't supported (D-05) |
| a person approves, verified saving, calculated saving | "autonomous", "auto-buy", "saves you X%" | Product rule 8; no real savings data |
