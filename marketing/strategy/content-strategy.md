# Content strategy (company level, draft)

**These pillars are a proposal derived from the approved website and LinkedIn copy. Approve or change them before
they drive a calendar.** Upstream skill for revision: `content-strategy`; per-product pillars come from each
product's messaging matrix.

## Objective

Build awareness and credibility for Iron Systems as an engineering-first builder of AI and software that works in
production, and create demand for its products, starting with ProcurePilot. Organic channels only.

## Proposed pillars

| Pillar | What it covers | Why (source) | Formats |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. AI that works in production | How we evaluate, monitor and keep people in the loop; where AI helps and where it doesn't | Website AI section; principle "AI where it helps" | LinkedIn posts and carousels, website articles |
| 2. Operations problems, solved | Concrete back-office problems (documents, approvals, re-typed data) and how systems fix them | Website services "The problem" lines | Problem/solution posts, use-case pages, short video |
| 3. Engineering first | Process, architecture decisions, build quality, ownership | Website process section; principle "Built to last"; guideline D6 "principle series" | Carousels, founder/engineer perspectives |
| 4. Products in action | ProcurePilot (and later products): real screens, one workflow at a time | Website products section; demo videos | Product education posts, reels, landing pages |

Ratio to test (hypothesis): roughly 40% pillar 2, 25% pillar 1, 20% pillar 4, 15% pillar 3. Revisit with data.

## Repurposing chain

One source asset → derivatives, adapted per channel (never pasted unchanged):

```
Website article or use-case page (EN + AR)
 ├─ LinkedIn: text post + carousel (company page; founder repost optional)
 ├─ Instagram: carousel + reel script
 ├─ Facebook: post adapted from LinkedIn, more visual
 └─ Video: 30–60 s short (9:16) and a 16:9 cut for the website / LinkedIn
```

## Cadence

Start with what can be sustained at quality. Proposed: 2 LinkedIn posts a week, 1 Instagram/Facebook post a week,
1 website piece a month. Set the actual cadence per campaign in `campaigns/<id>/content-calendar.md`.

## Open decisions

- Target regions and Arabic dialect for social and video (D-07)
- Whether founders/engineers post personally (needs their consent and voice guide)
- Whether the website gets a blog/articles section (a website change: needs approval and a website task)
