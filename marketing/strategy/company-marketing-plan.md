## Message

- **Lead:** Iron Systems builds AI solutions for businesses, designed, built and run in production (CO-02, LinkedIn About).
- **Foundation:** four disciplines, one engineering team (CO-03). Problem first · AI where it helps · Built to last (CO-06).
- **Proof:** we build and run our own AI product, ProcurePilot: Arabic document reading, comparison, a person approving every step (CO-08, CO-09, CO-10 and ProcurePilot's approved claims).
- **Never:** clients, results, team size, years, certifications, offices (not in evidence); "the only Arabic…" (D-13).
