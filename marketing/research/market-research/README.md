# Market research

One file per research question: `YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md`, started from `templates/` where one fits.

Each file states the research question, the sources (logged in `../source-log.md`), findings with confidence
(high / medium / low), strategic implications and open questions. Market sizes and statistics need a citable
primary or credible secondary source; never estimate a number and present it as found. Workflow: `workflows/B-market-and-competitor-research.md`.
