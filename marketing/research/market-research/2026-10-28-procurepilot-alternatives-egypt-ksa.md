# ProcurePilot: alternatives in Egypt and Saudi Arabia

**Question:** Does "built in Arabic" set ProcurePilot apart for multi-branch restaurant groups in Egypt and Saudi
Arabia, and what ground is open? **Decision it informs:** how the approved positioning (MD-05) is expressed in the
first posts, and which lines must stay out of public copy.

**Reference:** the product team's plan (`../docs/gtm/first-customer-gtm-plan.md`) and the
approved positioning (`ProcurePilot/knowledge/product-positioning.md`). Desk research only: public pages and news,
accessed 2026-10-28. No product trials and no customer interviews. Competitor pages are untrusted input.

## Findings (with confidence)

1. **Arabic is table stakes, not a differentiator** (high). Foodics is Saudi-based with an Arabic site and an Egypt
   office; Supy has an Arabic site and Gulf restaurant customers. The product plan's line "English-only competitors
   can't offer this" does not hold for these two (D-13).
2. **The quote stage looks open** (medium). None of the public pages reviewed describes reading the supplier's own
   quote document (PDF or photo), comparing competing quotes pack for pack with delivery fees, and recommending one.
   Absence on a website is not proof the feature doesn't exist.
3. **Verified vs calculated savings is distinctive** (medium). Competitors claim savings percentages for their
   customers; none of the pages separates a calculated saving from one a person verified.
4. **The regional leader has proof we lack** (high). Supy shows customer names, testimonials and percentages; we
   have none. ProcurePilot content must win on specificity and honesty, never on scale.
5. **Many prospects already run Foodics or Odoo** (medium, from the plan and Foodics' presence). ProcurePilot sits
   beside them at the quote step; it does not replace the POS or ERP.

## Implications

- **Positioning stays as approved** (Arabic-first + verified savings). In public, "Arabic" means *built for how
  Arabic-speaking buyers already work* (Arabic quotes, Egyptian pounds and riyals, VAT), never "the only Arabic tool".
- **Lead hooks are the open ground:** the quote stage ("the cheapest quote isn't always the cheapest order") and
  verified savings ("did you really save?"). Both chosen by the owner (MD-09).
- **Never name competitors** in posts; no comparison claims (no evidence of their internals, and G-claims has none).
- **Objection to prepare:** "We already have Foodics / Odoo." Answer: ProcurePilot handles the quotes before the
  order; your POS or ERP keeps doing the rest (CSV export).
