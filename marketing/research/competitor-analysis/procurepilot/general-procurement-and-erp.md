## Implications for us

- "Comparing quotes" alone is not unique (Odoo can do it). What is distinct: **the quote is read from what the supplier actually sent**, compared **pack for pack with delivery fees**, and the **saving is verified** afterwards.
- For Egyptian prospects on Odoo, ProcurePilot is not a replacement ERP; CSV export covers the hand-off (SRC-012).
